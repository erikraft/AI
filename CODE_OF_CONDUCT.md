# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment
We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone participating in the ErikrafT AI project, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Give and accept feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid personal attacks.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Insults, personal attacks, or deliberate intimidation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Spam, bad-faith disruption, or other conduct inappropriate for a collaborative project.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation for behavior they consider inappropriate. Reports should include relevant context and links when possible. Contact the repository owner through the contact options available on GitHub; do not publish sensitive personal information in public issues.

Reports will be handled as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible. Enforcement decisions may reflect the severity and pattern of the behavior.

## Scope
This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-related community interactions.

This document is inspired by common open-source community standards, including the Contributor Covenant. It does not replace applicable law or GitHub's policies.
